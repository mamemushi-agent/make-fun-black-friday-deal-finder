How DealScope checks a deal

  1. Read, not scraped from ads. Each price was read on the store's own page or in a deals source that names the store — the source is printed on every card.
  2. The maths must add up. The discount on a card is computed from the two prices on that card, never copied from a banner.
  3. Verdicts say the quiet part. A "comparable value" nobody paid is labelled a soft reference price, a 7% markdown is called routine, and a price that was beaten before is not called a low.
  4. Stores are compared. When the same product is on sale at several stores, the detail page lines them up side by side — the cheapest shelf wins the badge.
